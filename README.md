# Cratered Labs

hii :3, welcome to Cratered Labs, the group behind Lunar OS and YTSTAB

## What we work on

- **Lunar OS** — a browser-based dashboard/"OS" with a built-in Scramjet proxy, live TV, games, files, and a bunch of other stuff bundled in
- **YTSTAB** — a single-file Scramjet ad blocker mod, split out of Lunar OS (used to be Lunar Shield) so anyone can use it standalone

More stuff might show up here eventually, who knows

## Why Cratered Labs

small team (rn its just me lol), no funding, no weird monetization, just projects we actually wanted to make and figured other people might find useful too

## Philosophy

- keep stuff small and readable when we can, if you can read the whole file in a few minutes thats a win
- no remote code loading, no telemetry, no sneaky analytics bolted on
- open source, MIT licensed unless said otherwise
- we will never charge anything related to our projects
